Mochi Merge: Kawaii Café — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Mochi Merge: Kawaii Café ("the game") is a single-player puzzle game. This policy explains what the game does and does not do with your information.

Information we collect

None. The game does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools in the game.

Information stored on your device

The game saves a small amount of gameplay data in your device's local storage so it is there the next time you play: your best score, your chosen buddy, your sound on/off setting, and whether you have seen the welcome screen. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game.

Third parties

The game's files are delivered by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses when the game's files are downloaded; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The game itself makes no requests to any other third-party service.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects privacy (for example, if ads are added in a future version), this page will be updated before that version is released.
